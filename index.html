Finance module

Your earnings,
always at hand.

Stop chasing invoices in email threads. SoundSheet turns your logged work days into professional invoices in one tap — then tracks who's paid and who hasn't.

  • Generate invoices & quotes from logged days
  • Monthly earnings overview with visual breakdowns
  • Export CSV for your accountant or tax return
  • Track paid, pending, and overdue invoices
  • Share PDFs via email, AirDrop, or Messages
SFX Notes

Catalog every sound.
Every time.

Field recording sessions generate hundreds of takes. SFX Notes lets you log metadata on the spot — so your library is organized and production-ready when you get back.

  • Log subject, distance, mic setup, and take notes
  • Mark status on each take — right on set or in the field
  • Import session lists via CSV, export UCS-ready metadata
  • Organize by category, session, or recording day
Scout & Prep

Before the first
slate drops.

Scout locations, log noise risks, and attach photos — then build your Prep Day checklist so you arrive on set ready. Everything documented, nothing forgotten.

  • Log location notes with noise risk: Low, Medium, High, Critical
  • Attach photos directly from the scout to each note
  • Build Prep Day checklists with gear and task items
  • Save templates and reuse them across similar jobs
Log Takes

Every take,
logged in real time.

No clipboards. No spreadsheets. SoundSheet puts a fast, distraction-free take logger right on set — so your takes are tracked as the camera rolls, ready for post before the day wraps.

  • Open from the Days screen — on set in seconds
  • Circled, OK, NG, False Start, Hold — one tap per status
  • Timecode auto-filled from the clock, auto file name per take
  • Export Sound Report CSV for the dialogue editor or post team
