1. Who we are

SoundSheet is an iOS application developed and operated by cactuzz sound ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy applies to the SoundSheet mobile app (iOS / iPadOS) and the website soundsheet.app.

3. iCloud & CloudKit

SoundSheet uses Apple CloudKit to sync your private data across your Apple devices. This sync occurs between your devices via your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. cactuzz sound has no access to your private CloudKit data.

iCloud sync is optional. If you are not signed in to iCloud or disable iCloud Drive for SoundSheet in iOS Settings, all data remains only on-device.

4. Gear Page (Web Publish)

The optional Gear Website subscription allows you to publish your gear inventory as a publicly accessible web page at soundsheet.app/gear/your-slug.

When you activate this feature and enable publishing, the following data is written to Apple's CloudKit public database — meaning it becomes publicly readable by anyone on the internet:

  • Your display name (as entered in App Settings)
  • Gear item names, brands, categories, and quantities
  • Photos attached to gear items (if any)
  • A URL slug derived from your name
  • The timestamp of the last sync

Important: Published gear page data is public. Do not include sensitive personal information (e.g. home address, private serial numbers you wish to keep confidential) in fields that are synced to the public database.

You can unpublish your gear page at any time from within the App. This marks your page as private and deletes all associated public records from CloudKit immediately.

If you cancel the Gear Website subscription, your page is automatically unpublished and all public records are removed.

5. Advertising

The free tier of SoundSheet displays banner advertisements provided by Apple's App Store Ad Network. These ads may be personalised based on your Apple Advertising preferences, which you can manage in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Purchasing the Remove Ads in-app purchase permanently disables all advertising within the App for the purchasing Apple ID.

We do not use any third-party advertising SDKs (e.g. Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network). No advertising identifiers (IDFA) are accessed by cactuzz sound.

6. In-App Purchases

SoundSheet offers in-app purchases (Remove Ads — one-time; Gear Website — monthly subscription) processed exclusively through Apple's App Store / StoreKit. cactuzz sound never receives, stores, or processes your payment card details. All billing is handled by Apple and subject to Apple's Terms of Sale.

Purchase receipts and entitlement checks are verified locally on-device via StoreKit 2. No receipt data is transmitted to cactuzz sound servers.

7. Analytics & diagnostics

SoundSheet does not integrate any third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase, Mixpanel, Amplitude). No behavioural analytics or usage telemetry is collected by cactuzz sound.

If you have opted in to Share iPhone & Watch Analytics in iOS Settings, Apple may share anonymised, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics with us through App Store Connect. This data contains no personally identifiable information and is controlled by Apple's privacy framework.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Apple / iCloud: As described in sections 3–6 above, necessary to provide core app functionality.
  • Legal obligation: If required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority.

There are no data brokers, marketing partners, or analytics vendors who receive your data.

9. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the App or until you delete it. You are in full control:

  • Delete individual records at any time from within the App
  • Unpublish your gear page to remove all public CloudKit records immediately
  • Delete the App from your device — local data (UserDefaults, Core Data) is erased by iOS
  • Manage or delete iCloud data at icloud.com or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage

To request deletion of any data we may hold, contact us at hello@cactuzzsound.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access: Request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal data
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data

Since all data is stored in your iCloud account, most of these rights are exercisable directly through your iOS device and icloud.com. For any data held by cactuzz sound, contact hello@cactuzzsound.com.

California residents (CCPA): We do not sell personal information. You have the right to know what data is collected and to request deletion. Contact us at the email above to exercise these rights. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

11. Children

SoundSheet is designed for working audio professionals and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
